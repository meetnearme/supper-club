Before you pull up a chair

A few
good questions.

What does a seat cost?
Pricing is being finalized. We’re planning for dinner at approximately $45–$75 per person, plus a $15 hosting fee, for an estimated $60–$90 total. Your invitation will include the exact dinner-inclusive ticket price and what’s included before you confirm. Requesting an invitation is free.
Is this for me?
If you’re building, leading, making, or rethinking what comes next, we’d love to hear from you. Founders, operators, independent professionals, creative business owners, tradespeople, and people doing meaningful local work are all welcome. Curiosity matters more than your job title.
Why request an invitation?
There are only 12–20 seats, and the mix of people matters. A few details help your hosts bring together different industries, experiences, and perspectives. It’s about a high-quality table, not exclusivity for its own sake. A request doesn’t guarantee a seat.
What happens after I request?
Your request goes to Brian and Ben for personal review. A request is not an invitation or a reserved seat. If you’re invited, your hosts will privately email you secret ticket link with the final price, start time, and dinner details. Please confirm within 48 hours of receiving your invitation so we can finalize the table.
Can you accommodate dietary needs?
Tell us about any allergies or dietary needs in your request. We’ll check with the venue and confirm what’s possible before you commit to your seat.
Do I need to prepare anything?
No slides, no pitch, and no homework. Come with an open mind, a little curiosity about the evening’s topic, and a willingness to listen as well as share. The dinner lasts approximately 2½–3 hours; the exact start time will be in your invitation.
