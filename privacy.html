Privacy

What we keep,
and why.

Updated October 8, 2026

Who we are

The Redding Supper Club is a dinner hosted by Brian Feister of Meet Near Me and Ben Loschen of SmartLemon. This page explains what this website collects and what we do with it.

What you tell us

When you request an invitation, we collect your email address and anything else you choose to share: your name, what you’re working on, a question you’d bring, and allergies or dietary needs. Netlify, our web host, stores your request and emails it to your hosts.

We use it to review requests, plan the table and the meal, and contact you about the dinner. We share allergy and dietary details only as needed to prepare your meal.

What we measure

We use PostHog to understand how people find and use this site: the pages you visit, the site or ad that sent you (including any campaign tags in the link), your browser and device type, and an approximate location based on your IP address. PostHog keeps a random identifier in your browser so visits can be counted. It also records how visitors move through the page, such as scrolling and clicks, so we can see where the site is confusing. Anything you type into the form is hidden from those recordings, and your form answers are not sent to PostHog.

Advertising

We advertise the dinner on Facebook and Instagram and use Meta’s pixel to measure those ads and show them to people likely to be interested. The pixel tells Meta which pages you visit here, which buttons you click, and whether you request an invitation, along with technical details such as your IP address and browser, and it stores a Meta cookie in your browser.

When you request an invitation, Meta may also receive scrambled (hashed) copies of the email address and name you enter, so it can match the visit to a Meta account. Your other answers, including what you’re working on and any dietary needs, are not sent to Meta.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal information. Netlify and PostHog handle it on our behalf, and only to provide their services to us. Meta receives only what’s described above, to measure and deliver our ads.

How long we keep it

We keep invitation requests only as long as we need them to organize the dinner, and we delete yours sooner if you ask.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what you’ve sent us, reply to any email from your hosts or message Brian or Ben on LinkedIn. You can manage how Meta uses information from sites like this one in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can block analytics and Meta’s pixel with your browser’s tracking protection or a content blocker. This site doesn’t change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals.

Changes

If we change this page, we’ll update the date at the top.
